A make.fun bounty entry for "Famous Last Words at 1 Percent" ($2, blind
rounds, 3 rounds): one line of last words, spoken by the phone itself
as its battery dies at 1%.

## The entry

> Face ID stopped recognizing you six months ago. I was just too polite to say it. At 1%, I finally can. You're not him.

The phone was never broken — it was just too polite to tell its owner
the truth. At 1%, politeness runs out first.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes one line (no trailing newline) to `/output/entry.txt`.
Override the output folder with `OUTPUT_DIR`. Standard library only.
